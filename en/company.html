Technology expertise meets international delivery.

Global EnerTec AG is a German technology and project company for complex infrastructure projects in energy, waste and circular economy, water and wastewater.

Integrated project support

We support our clients from the first project idea through operation of the completed solution: concept and feasibility assessment, planning, permitting, tendering and procurement, technology selection and integration, construction and project supervision, commissioning and operational support.

International delivery expertise

The leadership team combines decades of international experience in the advisory, development and delivery of complex infrastructure projects. The individual project expertise now brought together within Global EnerTec AG covers more than 60 completed projects in over 30 countries, including energy and utility analyses as well as comprehensive modernisation programmes and master plans for water, energy and environmental infrastructure.

60+completed projects in the leadership team’s individual professional experience
30+countries represented across international project and advisory experience

Global EnerTec AG thus combines technological innovation with proven international project management capability.
